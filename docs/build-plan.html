Sprint 5 – Payroll Done

Payroll runs once a month for every eligible person, in five steps. Each step locks what came before it.

Open→Month close→Dry run→Verified→Approved→Published

Compensation page

  • Salary structure versions (Version 1, Version 2 …) with every rate editable: basic %, HRA %, PF, ESI, medical, gratuity, bonus, variable pay, TDS rate.
  • One default version. Payroll maps people to a version from a date. A version change in the middle of a month blends both calculations by days.
  • Pay bands by city and grade, with copy from another city. Labour welfare fund rules by state. Headcount and payroll budgets by property.

Month close

  • Lists pending leave and regularizations grouped by approver. Payroll can remind each approver or send everything to the attendance desk and all approvers.
  • Attendance summary download.
  • Lock attendance stays disabled while anything is pending. Reject pending and lock closes each one with the note “Closed at payroll cut-off”.
  • After the lock, regularizations for that month are refused unless Payroll Manager opens an exception. Late corrections are paid as arrears.
  • Cut-off is the calendar month, or the 26th to the 25th when set.

Payroll run

  • Pilot: optionally run a small chosen group first.
  • Dry run: one row per eligible person: active, left in or after this month, or with pay lines waiting. The row shows paid days, earned gross, statutory items, ledger lines and net.
    • Flags: on hold, paid days edited, manual adjustment, missing punch, net change over 15%, zero or negative net, arrears or claims, recoveries, joined this month.
    • Filters and paging handle thousands of rows.
    • Paid days, other earning and other deduction can be edited on screen or in a downloaded sheet that is uploaded back. Every edit needs a reason.
  • Run checks: total net against last month (within ±5%), people moving more than 15%, zero net, holds, ledger lines, manual changes, new joiners.
  • Verify → Approve → Publish by the roles that hold those rights. Publishing:
    • releases payslips and writes TDS history
    • carries a negative net into next month as a deduction
    • locks the month
  • Large runs are calculated in the background (River job), so the page never times out.

Pay lines (ledger)

  • Salary advance: Permanent only, 1 to 12 instalments. Skip next instalment, or recover the balance in one go.
  • Salary deduction: reason, instalments, employee heard (Payment of Wages Act).
  • Arrear: for past months, with payslip text; auto-raised by a back-dated salary change.
  • Claim: Travel, Mobile and internet, Other, with the bill; up to 15 days after the last working day.
  • Salary hold: a reason from the Hold master. Release pays every held month.
  • Bank change: holds salary until Payroll verifies it.
  • Upcoming pay lines: the next six months, with totals and a download.

Payslips and outputs

  • Payslip setup: company name, address, logo (500 KB max), accent colour, CIN/PAN/TAN/PF/ESI codes, footer, and whether to show employer contributions.
  • Payslip with the amount in words and a masked PAN. Viewed and printed or saved as PDF from the browser.
  • Retainer and contractual staff get no payslip document.
  • Payroll register, bank advice (Excel) and cost report by city, department, type and grade.
  • My pay: payslips for every published month.

Employee file

One page per person: personal, bank, tax, attendance and pay. It shows readiness for the payroll month (missing bank details, PAN, UAN, pending changes) and open items, with who each is waiting on.

Left over from sprint 4, done here

  • Overtime and comp-off credit from the work policy settings.
  • Locking regularizations once the month is closed.
  • Blocking casual and privilege leave taken back to back (the clubbing setting).
  • Showing who else in the team is on leave on a leave request.

Checked against the spec's golden values (Gurugram, Delhi, Mumbai) and the guide's month example: gross 30,000 with an advance, a cash shortage and an arrear nets 38,166 in November and 33,827 in December.

Sprint 6 – Income tax and statutory files Done

Income tax and Form 16

  • Declaration window and proof window set by Payroll: dates, or forced open or closed.
  • Employee picks a regime (New is the default) and declares under 80C, 80CCD(1B), 80D, 80E, 80G, 80TTA, 24(b), LTA, and HRA with rent, landlord name and PAN. Employee PF counts under 80C automatically.
  • Proof uploads with remarks. Payroll approves an amount per proof. Finalising, allowed only after the proof window closes, keeps approved amounts only. Any extra tax is spread over the months left.
  • Monthly salary TDS (Section 192) recomputed each run and frozen once published. Retainers pay a flat TDS rate (194J). Contractual staff have none.
  • TDS deposits per month (date, BSR code, challan) with the due date (7th, or 30 April for March) and an overdue flag.
  • 24Q and 26Q quarterly data download, with the receipt number recorded.
  • Form 16: Part A (employer, PAN/TAN, quarterly receipts, challans) and Part B (computation). Provisional during the year, final after March; released by a payroll approver; downloadable as one printable file.

Statutory and insurance files

  • Insurance enrolment and removal (weekly, date range): people on employer medical cover only.
  • PF enrolment and exit: UAN status, exit reason, Aadhaar, PAN, bank, EPF wage.
  • ESI enrolment and exit: IP status, wages, dispensary city.
  • ECR PF contribution text file (fields joined by #~#): EPF, EPS and EDLI wages, NCP days, a “UAN missing” remark.
  • ESI contribution file: IP number, days paid, wages, reason codes.
  • Range files can skip people already sent. Missing UAN or IP numbers are flagged. Downloads need the download right and respect data scope.
  • Professional tax is not built (deferred in the spec), but the structure leaves room to add it.

Sprint 7 – Separation, full and final, employee dues Done

One case tracker for every exit, run through one approval flow called Separation.

Show-cause (absconding)→Approval→Notice→Clearance→Full and final→Letters→Closed

Separation cases

  • Types: Resignation, Termination, Absconding, Contract end, Retirement, Death. Employees start only a resignation; HR Admin, HRBP and HRBP Corporate start any type. One open case per person.
  • Resignation LWD = date given + notice from the policy. The approver can bring it earlier, never later, and waive or recover the shortfall. Withdrawal is allowed within 7 days.
  • Termination needs a note: pay in lieu, serve the notice, or none (misconduct).
  • Absconding: 7 days absent in a row, then 2 show-cause letters 3 days apart, then approval. Salary is held automatically. “Reported back” cancels the case.
  • Clearance by the reporting manager, the HRBP (assets and uniform checked against records, ID card, exit interview) and Finance (advances, claims). Items are configurable.
  • Exit interview: main reason, 5 ratings, would recommend. Private to HR.
  • Letters: termination, relieving, experience, no dues, show-cause. Closing makes the person inactive the day after LWD, with a rehire flag.

Full and final

  • The final month's salary is paid in the normal run for the LWD month.
  • Leave encashment: basic ÷ 26 × PL balance at LWD, floored to half a day.
  • Gratuity: at least 4.75 years of service (any service on death), basic × 15 ÷ 26 × years, capped at Rs 20 lakh.
  • Notice pay or recovery. Recoveries scheduled after LWD are brought forward, and so are approved earnings. Held salary is shown for information.
  • Pay month within 45 days of LWD. Approved by net amount. Settlement lines go to the ledger.

Employee dues

  • Stores: a central store and one per HRBP. Additions, transfers, issues, returns and write-offs. Each HRBP sees opening, additions, issued, returns and closing per item.
  • Assets with serial numbers: Available, Issued, Damaged or Lost; never deleted.
  • Uniform: 50% recovered in the issue month and 50% the next, refunded 6 months after the last deduction.
  • One place for advances, holds and deductions. Since 12 Nov: opening advances from Excel, and Payroll opens on Advances.

Decisions taken, and questions for you

Decisions (tell me to change any)

  • Payslips and Form 16 are printable pages: download from the browser as PDF. No server PDF engine to run.
  • Bank advice and register are Excel files. ECR is the text format EPFO expects; rows without a UAN are left out of the text file and flagged.
  • Finalising tax does not add separate recovery lines: once only approved amounts count, the monthly TDS recalculation spreads any extra tax over the months left.
  • Payroll cut-off defaults to the calendar month; 26th–25th is a setting.
  • Nobody approves a request they raised, including HR Admin with the override (spec §7; your decision, 3 Nov). A step whose approver raised the request, is the person it is about, has left, or is not named moves to the next approver; at the last step it goes to the escalation role, then HR Admin, then the CHRO.
  • Surcharge on income tax and professional tax are not built, as the spec says.
  • Company TAN, PAN, CIN, PF and ESI codes are placeholders in Company details until you fill them in. Form 16 release is blocked until TAN and PAN are set.
  • The open payroll month is the month after the last approved run. Before the first run it is last month; Payroll settings can set the first month.
  • Approve and publish need the Payroll Manager (or CHRO, HR Admin). The Payroll executive prepares and verifies.
  • Reminders at month close are recorded with the time and audited; no SMS or email is sent yet (no provider is connected).
  • Overtime (when a work policy turns it on) and the night shift allowance are paid as earnings lines, outside PF and ESI wages, like arrears. With “needs approval”, only extra hours on deviations marked OK count. The quarterly cap uses earlier months' runs.
  • Comp-off is credited when a weekly-off or holiday deviation is marked OK, and expires after 90 days unless the leave plan sets another period.
  • Separation letters are generated from built-in wording with the company and employee details; HR can print or save them as PDF. Editing the letter wording in the app is not built yet.
  • The punch connector takes a simple JSON push (employee ID, time, IN or OUT). Each vendor's own protocol needs a small bridge once the vendor is known.
  • Reports preview the first 500 rows on screen; the Excel download has all rows.
  • Leave clubbing blocks casual and privilege leave on back-to-back dates. A weekly off or holiday between them still counts as a gap, so they are allowed.

Questions (not blocking; defaults used until you answer)

  • Which bank do salaries go through? Each bank wants its own bank-advice layout. Until then the file is a generic Excel sheet.
  • Which insurer handles medical cover? Their enrolment file layout may differ from the spec's columns.
  • Which punch-machine vendor? The connector takes a standard push format until then.
  • Should notice shortfall use gross ÷ days in the month (as coded) or gross ÷ 30? Spec 21 #12 says confirm with business.
  • ECR: the spec caps EPS wages at the PF ceiling (₹25,000, per the ruling), so EPS is ₹2,083 at the cap. EPFO's own EPS ceiling is ₹15,000 (₹1,250). Built as the spec says; confirm before the first upload.
  • Insurance file: there is no opt-out field yet, so everyone on employer medical cover is included. Cover type is “Employee and family” when a verified medical nomination lists family.

Joiner self-fill link

Owner's request of 11 Nov 2026: the new joiner fills their own joining details and uploads their documents, from a link HR sends to the email or mobile number already on the offer.

  • Who fills what: Onboarding › Form setup has a Filled by column (Joiner / HR) for every field and document. Personal, Identity and statutory, Bank and Benefits are the joiner's by default; Job, Location and reporting, Hiring and Compensation are HR's; every document is the joiner's. System fields, pay and fields that pick from the company's lists stay HR's. Store migration 006_joiner_link sets the defaults.
  • Sending: the Joiner's link panel in the joining dialog, whose text follows the link and the offer: Email the link (then Email it again; company mailbox over SMTP, SMTP_* settings), Copy link, Share on WhatsApp; under them Give a code by phone, Send a new link, Stop the link; after a submit only Send back to the joiner with a note. A live link made in another tab is not shown, and replacing it says the joiner's link stops working. The pipeline row shows Link sent, Link opened or Joiner submitted, and waits on the joiner while they have the link.
  • HR's joining dialog: HR's own fields first (required empty ones highlighted), then the joiner's, marked From joiner (entered by the joiner on any link) or Joiner fills (still empty). HR's Save sends only what HR changed, so the joiner's newer values stay; Refresh brings in the joiner's latest and keeps what HR typed.
  • The joiner's page (/join/…, no sign-in, built for phones): a 6-digit code by email (without email, Ask HR to call me), then the form by section with plain hints, documents (camera photos or PDF), Save and continue later (keeps every good value and lists what to fix), Submit to HR (one list of everything to fix, field by field), a thank-you page with the joining date and the originals to bring. What the joiner typed survives a session that ends (kept in the tab until the next code).
  • Security: the link token (32 random bytes) and the code are stored only as hashes; one live link per offer, 7 days; the code lasts 10 minutes, 5 wrong tries lock it, 15 wrong codes on a link in all lock the link (a new code never gives tries back); every try and every code email is counted in one atomic update, so calls sent together cannot pass the limits; the session after the code covers that one link for 60 minutes and is not a sign-in; the link stops when the offer is declined, the candidate closed or Payroll verifies; the public pages return only the company, the joiner's name, offer number, type, joining date and the joiner's own fields and documents; rate limits per address (behind a proxy, the address its X-Forwarded-For names, TRUSTED_PROXIES), and made-up links never block a real one; the token is masked in the API log; every step in the audit log.
  • Without email: HR shares the link by Copy or WhatsApp and gives the code on the phone (shown once to HR). HR's bell says when the joiner opens the link or presses Ask HR to call me (at most once in 10 minutes).
  • Unchanged: HR can still correct every field and fills HR's fields; HR's Save creates the Employee ID when everything mandatory is in; Payroll verifies as before.
  • Tests: Go API tests TestJoinerLink, TestJoinerLinkWithoutEmail, TestJoinerFormSetup, and the review fixes TestJoinerCodeGuessBurst, TestJoinerNewCodeKeepsLinkTries, TestJoinerCodeSendBurst, TestJoinerRateLimitBehindProxy, TestJoinerTokenNotLogged, TestJoinerLinkStopsWhenHireEnds, TestJoinerOpenedTellsHRWithoutEmail; store TestJoinerDefaults, mail package tests (a local SMTP server); browser steps in tests/e2e/forms.mjs (HR emails the link, the joiner fills and submits at 390 px, HR saves).

Type to find a person, everywhere

  • Looks like a dropdown: the box shows "Name · EMP-1023" with a clear (×) button; clicking or typing opens the matches (name, code, and designation · department · location). ↑/↓, Enter and Esc work. The best 20 show, with "Keep typing to narrow" when there are more. Pages that had "All employees" or "Keep current manager" keep it as the first option.

Exact first, then a word's start, then inside a word, then a typo.
  • Where: Attendance (records, calendar, regularization), Approvals (new request employee and receiving manager, manager has left, joining verification L1/L2, Check a flow), Access control (map a role, check a person), Onboarding (hiring manager, L1/L2), Separation, Employee file, Employee dues and Inventory (employee search), Work policies › Exceptions, Compensation › offer letter preview, My pay leave form.
  • Server: GET /api/employees?q= inside the caller's scope and filters, best match first; a word search first, then everyone in scope scored for typos and initials when that finds fewer than 20 (about 25 ms for 3,000 people). Same scoring in the browser for lists a page already has.

Uniform sizes and the uniform refund policy

Owner's request of 13 Nov 2026: record each employee's uniform sizes (Shirt / T-shirt, trouser waist and length, shoes), track uniform by size, recover it from salary and refund it after some months of employment as per policy.

  • Sizes on the employee: a Uniform sizes block on the employee record and on the payroll file (Uniform tab). HR, the HRBP and Payroll (whoever edits employee details or manages dues) change them; every change is in the change history and the audit log. Lists (XS–4XL, waist 26–44, length 28–36, UK 4–12) are kept by HR Admin in Employee dues › Setup.
  • New joiners: Form setup has a Uniform section (four optional joining fields, filled by the joiner, switchable to HR); the sizes copy to the record on joining.
  • Bulk: Bulk update › Uniform sizes (current data, upload), optional size columns on Import employees, and a Uniform sizes group in Download all details.
  • Issue by size: each uniform item says what it is sized by (shirt, trouser, shoes, no size). Issuing shows the employee's sizes and pre-fills each item's size, which can be changed; the size is kept on each issued line and shown in the issue list, on the record and in My view.
  • Uniform sizes summary (Employee dues): active employees by size for each item, trousers by waist and length, by location and department, with who has no sizes, and an Excel download for ordering.
  • Refund: the split is unchanged (50% in the issue month by default, the rest the next month). The refund basis is either N months after the last deduction (as before, the default) or after N months of employment (the month they complete them, or the month after the last deduction if later). Paid only to someone still employed then: a leaver's refund is cancelled, the recovery stands, and the full and final shows the refund as not paid (it used to bring it forward). Undo inactive brings it back.
  • Data: migration 008 adds the uniform_size collection, the four Form setup fields and the size type of items named shirt, trouser or shoe. Existing issues and refund months do not change.
  • Tests: Go TestUniformSizes, TestUniformJoining, TestUniformMigration; browser tests/e2e/uniform.mjs (npm run uniform).
