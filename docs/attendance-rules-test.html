Fixed on 24 Nov 2026 after the first run (3 rows were wrong, all now match):
  • Rows 14 and 18: arriving more than 30 min early (09:29 on a 10:00 shift) gave a Late mark, measured against the 09:00 General shift. Now arriving before the planned start is never a late mark; the day follows the hours.
  • Row 42: when the previous day broke the longest-gap limit (10:00–20:01), its 20:01 out punch was carried into the next day and turned a correct 10:00–18:00 day into a missing punch. Now an out punch stays on its own day unless it is past midnight.
How this was tested. One employee on a 10:00–18:00 shift (other shifts where the rule needs them), in a copy of the sample data, never the live data. The work policy was set the way HR sets it, the roster through the roster cell, and the punches were sent through the punch-machine connection that live machines use. Each day was then read back from the HR attendance month, the roster deviations list and, for pay, the payroll row. The expected result in every line was written from the rule’s help text before the system was run, and was not changed afterwards. All numbers are test values (for example 2 h for the deviation allowances and 30 min for the punch window); you set the real ones. Days are in last month, which payroll has not paid. The same cases now run as an automatic check (backend/internal/api/attrules_report_test.go).

Mismatches (0)

Worth knowing (these match the rules as written, but may surprise)

  • Leaving even a minute early is a half day when the full-day hours equal the shift length (8 h on 10:00–18:00): 17:59 out is a Half day, and the early-out grace (10 min) and an allowed early check-out add no hours (lines on Working hours and Deviation allowances).
  • More than 1 hour late with a full 8 h worked is Present with no late mark: past the 60 min late-arrival window it becomes a Planned against Actual deviation for the manager instead. 59 min late is a late mark; 61 min late is not. With the 2 h late check-in allowance on, a 2 h 1 min late arrival is reported to HR and the manager but, under “No action”, is Present.
  • A jump at the early check-in allowance: with “time before the start: Not counted”, 09:30–17:30 counts 7 h 30 min (Half day) but 09:29–17:29 counts 8 h, because the setting applies only inside the 30 min allowance.
  • The punch window overrides the early check-in allowance: with a 30 min maximum early in punch, a punch 2 h before the start is never taken, so the 2 h early check-in allowance cannot be used. Keep the window wider than the allowance.
  • Labels: “No punch counts as Present” and “Missing punch counts as Present until regularized” give no loss of pay, but the day still reads “No punch” / “Missing in/out punch”. On the policy screen, Late marks allowed, After that limit, Early-out grace, Missing punch, No punch and If out punch is missing carry the ◦ mark (“not applied yet”), yet the system applies all of them.
  • Not tested: “Night shift counts on” (◦ on the policy screen, not used by the system: a night shift always counts on the day it starts); geo-fence, selfie, ways to punch, work from home and on-duty (not attendance-status rules).
