Saltstayz HRMS Go-Live Checklist

What we need from Saltstayz to run the HRMS in production · prepared 20 Nov 2026

The HRMS is built and tested. To switch it on for real staff we need the items below. Items marked Before go-live block the launch. Items marked First month can follow shortly after.

1. Hosting and access

What we needWhyWhen
A server or cloud machine for the HRMS (Linux; 4 CPU, 8 GB RAM, 100 GB disk is enough to start) and access for our teamRuns the application and databaseBefore go-live
A web address, e.g. hrms.saltstayz.com, with DNS pointed to the serverStaff and HR open the HRMS here; it gets an HTTPS certificateBefore go-live
Agreement on daily backups and who keeps them (cloud storage or a second disk)Employee and payroll records must be recoverableBefore go-live
Optional: Google Drive account for storing uploaded documentsOffer letters, IDs and payslips can live in your DriveFirst month

2. Realtime India attendance portal

What we needWhyWhen
Licence renewal: the current licence ends around 29 Nov 2026All punch data stops if it lapsesBefore go-live
A portal login for the HRMS (a separate user is best)The HRMS reads swipes and creates, updates and marks leavers on the portal automaticallyBefore go-live
Confirmation that the HRMS may add, edit and mark employees as left on the portalNew joiners reach the punch machines without manual entryBefore go-live
Which branch on the portal belongs to which property, and each machine's locationMaps the 40 machines to properties and clustersBefore go-live

3. Company and statutory details

What we needWhyWhen
Company PAN and TAN, PF establishment code, ESIC code, LWF registrations by statePayslips, challans, Form 16 and statutory filesBefore go-live
States where you have staff, and whether any charge Professional Tax (e.g. Maharashtra, Karnataka, West Bengal)PT is not yet calculated; we add it only if neededBefore go-live
Salary debit bank account (12-digit ICICI account) and confirmation of the ICICI bulk upload formatThe bank file for paying salariesBefore go-live
Name and designation of the Form 16 signatoryPrinted on Form 16First month

4. Your organisation's data

What we needWhyWhen
States, cities, clusters and all properties (the portal shows 38 branches)Everything (access, budgets, accommodation, machines) is organised by theseBefore go-live
Departments, designations, grades, shifts, roles and skillsRequired on every employee and offerBefore go-live
Employee master (Excel template provided): personal details, PAN, Aadhaar, UAN or "no existing UAN", ESIC no, bank, salary, attendance codeWithout a UAN or a PF choice, the HRMS holds that person's salaryBefore go-live
Opening leave balances, opening advances and uniforms issued (templates provided)So balances continue from your current recordsBefore go-live
Staff accommodation: each flat or PG, its cluster, beds, landlord, lease dates and monthly rentAccommodation cost by property and clusterFirst month
Monthly cost budget (CTC) and headcount budget per property and clusterCost and budget page, and the budget check on offersFirst month

5. Policies and approvals

What we needWhyWhen
Work policies (working days, weekly offs, grace time) and leave plans, and which apply to whomAttendance and leave are worked out from theseBefore go-live
Holiday calendar for each city for 2026 and 2027Holidays in attendance and payrollBefore go-live
Who approves what (shortlist, offer, joining, leave, regularisation, separation), and which processes HR may skipApproval flowsBefore go-live
List of HR admins and their roles, and which reports each role may seeAccess controlBefore go-live

6. Notifications

What we needWhyWhen
Email sending details (SMTP server, sender address such as hr@saltstayz.com)Joiner links, offer letters, alerts and payslip notices by emailBefore go-live
WhatsApp or SMS provider, and DLT registration for SMS templatesAttendance alerts and reminders on phoneFirst month
Who receives the daily manager attendance digestAttendance alertsFirst month

7. Launch plan

  1. Load the data above into a test copy and check it with HR (one week).
  2. Training for HR admins and payroll (half a day), plus a short guide for managers and employees.
  3. Parallel run: one payroll month calculated in both the old process and the HRMS; differences checked and signed off.
  4. Go-live date agreed; employees receive their login.
  5. Support during the first payroll month.

Questions on any item: contact the HRMS project team.
